Mediation Philosophy

Mediation is problem-solving,
not litigation warfare.

The role of a true neutral is not to force evaluative dictates or judge the past. It is to establish trust, confidence, and a safe, congenial environment, and energize counsel and parties to realistically evaluate risks and architect their own binding solutions.

True Impartiality
The mediator represents neither party. The sole objective is providing objective third-party feedback to keep negotiations dynamic, forward-moving, and realistic.
Statutory Privilege
All caucus discussions and financial working drafts are sealed under Florida statutory mediation privilege. Nothing enters the public court docket.
Tax & Financial Shield
Backed by an LL.M. in Taxation from the University of Miami, property allocations comply with IRC § 1041 to avoid catastrophic capital gains and IRS pitfalls.
Enforceable Finality
The resulting Mediated Settlement Agreement (MSA) is a binding legal contract prepared for immediate ratification by Florida circuit courts.

  • The Pandora's Box: $60,000 to $200,000+ per party in billable hours, forensic accounting, depositions, and runaway fee fights.

The Tell Methodology

4 Stages of Resolution:
from acute crisis to binding agreement

A structured, disciplined neutral process designed to prevent deadlock, posturing, and unnecessary delay.

01. Intake & Caucus Confidential separate sessions to evaluate key interests, emotional drivers, and realistic settlement zones.
02. Standstill Moratorium Locking the status quo: agreement to pause litigation filings, account freezes, and hostile actions during mediation.
03. Joint Settlement Rounds Structured negotiations in Broward / Miami executive conference facilities or Zoom, modeling buyouts and valuations.
04. Legal Execution Drafting an airtight, court-ratified Mediated Settlement Agreement (MSA) reviewed by respective counsel.

Professional Boundaries

Strict Neutrality &
Protection of Enterprise Value

The mediator takes no sides, imposes no judicial orders, and passes no moral judgments. The sole focus is capital preservation, family protection, and enforceable closure.

01 Equidistance from Both Parties: The mediator represents neither Side A nor Side B. The only client is a durable, binding settlement agreement.
02 Sanctity of Caucus: Everything shared in private caucus is strictly confidential and never revealed to the opposing side without explicit consent.
03 Voluntary Autonomy: Parties retain 100% control over agreement terms. No decisions are forced from above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers on Mediation

How mediation works, legal enforceability, and virtual conference options.

Is the mediated agreement legally binding?

Yes. A Mediated Settlement Agreement (MSA) signed by both parties and their counsel constitutes an enforceable contract under Florida law and is submitted to the court for immediate entry of a final order.

Are legal counsel encouraged to participate?

Yes. Meah Rothman Tell actively collaborates with trial and corporate counsel. The mediator works alongside attorneys to construct realistic settlement options while preserving legal protections.

Are virtual / remote sessions available?

Yes. A substantial number of commercial and family mediations are conducted via secure, encrypted Zoom videoconferencing, accommodating participants and counsel across Florida and nationwide.

Are services offered bilingually?

Yes. Meah Rothman Tell conducts mediations fluently in both English and Spanish (Bilingual: English & Español), providing seamless resolution for international and Hispanic families and business partners in South Florida.
